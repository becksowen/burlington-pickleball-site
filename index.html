What We're About

No Doubles.
No Weekends.
Just Singles.

This is a focused, competitive group for serious players who want consistent early morning play. If you're looking to improve your singles game, this is the spot.

🏓

Singles Only

We play 1v1 singles format exclusively. No recreational doubles. Come ready to compete and move your feet.

⭐

Rated Players

Open to players rated 3.0 to 4.0+. You should be comfortable with full-court singles play at a competitive pace.

📍

Local Community

Welcoming players from Burlington, Oakville, and Milton. We meet at B Town Courts — indoor, year-round.

🌅

Early Morning

Sessions run 6:00 AM to 7:00 AM, or 7:15 AM at the latest. Perfect for before-work play on weekdays.

📅

Weekdays Only

Monday through Friday, no exceptions. Weekends are off. Consistent early morning schedule, every week.
